How to Use the Online Decision Wheel

Making a decision takes less than ten seconds with our streamlined workflow:

  1. Input Your Choices: Type or paste your items into the text area. You can separate choices line by line or with commas. Alternatively, choose one of our quick presets like Yes/No, Food, Truth or Dare, or Numbers 1–6.
  2. Initiate the Spin: Press the "Spin the Wheel" button, tap/click anywhere on the wheel itself, flick the canvas on mobile, or hit the Spacebar on desktop.
  3. Watch the Realistic Deceleration: The wheel accelerates into an exhilarating spin before gradually coasting to a halt with authentic mechanical tick sounds and pointer deflection.
  4. Get Your Answer: The winning selection is dynamically highlighted on the wheel and announced in the high-contrast result banner with full screen-reader accessibility.
  5. Share or Re-Spin: Click "Share" to generate a link pre-loaded with your exact options to send to friends, colleagues, or stream viewers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the spin outcome truly random?

Yes. The wheel calculates its target position using modern pseudorandom number algorithms executed directly in your browser's JavaScript runtime. Each option receives an angular slice proportional to its share of the total options, ensuring every item has an equal probability of winning.

Can I spin the wheel without pressing the button?

Absolutely! You can click or tap directly on the wheel canvas, swipe/flick across the wheel on touchscreens, click the central tactile "SPIN" hub, or simply press the Spacebar on your keyboard anytime you are not actively typing in the text area.

Are my custom lists and options saved?

Yes. Whenever you type or edit options in the input box, they are automatically saved to your device's browser storage (HTML5 localStorage). When you revisit Vectric, your custom list is instantly restored without needing an account or login.

How do I share my wheel with others?

Click the Share button located right below the wheel controls. On supported mobile devices, it opens your native sharing sheet (WhatsApp, iMessage, Slack, etc.). On desktop, it automatically copies a shareable URL containing your preset options directly to your clipboard.

Is Vectric free to use?

Yes, Vectric Spin the Wheel is 100% free with unlimited spins, zero subscription fees, and no account requirements. It is a lightweight web utility built for everyone.
