What is "Spin the Wheel"?

Spin the Wheel – Random Decision Maker is a lightweight, responsive browser application designed to help individuals, groups, teachers, and teams pick randomly from a customized pool of options. Whether you can't decide where to eat dinner, need a fair volunteer picker for a classroom, or want to host a game night with friends, our wheel provides a quick, visual, and satisfying solution.

How Our Wheel Works

Our wheel utilizes high-resolution HTML5 Canvas rendering paired with cryptographic client-side mathematical randomness:

  • Physics-Inspired Deceleration: The spin mechanism applies a smooth quartic easing algorithm (ease-out) that simulates realistic angular momentum and friction before gently settling on the final winning slice.
  • True Unbiased Geometry: Each segment on the wheel occupies an angle proportional to the total number of entries (360° / N), guaranteeing that every option has an identical mathematical probability of being selected.
  • Top-Pointer Precision: The pointer is positioned at the 12 o'clock mark. When the wheel concludes its rotation, the relative angular displacement is calculated to declare the exact winning slice.

Key Features

  • Zero Lag & No Framework Bloat: Engineered entirely with pure semantic HTML5, pure CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript. No heavy third-party bundles, fonts, or external dependencies.
  • Privacy First: Your custom options are never uploaded to remote databases. All calculations and storage remain securely inside your local browser via localStorage.
  • Accessible to Everyone: Fully compatible with keyboard navigation, screen readers via WAI-ARIA live regions, and responsive across smartphones, tablets, and ultra-wide desktop monitors.
  • Convenient Presets: Pre-loaded with popular configurations including Yes/No, Food Picker, Truth or Dare, and Dice Numbers 1–6.
